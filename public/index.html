03 — The appWeek 14 of 31

Every session tells you why.

A — Today

Open it. Get one thing.

Today's session, how hard it should feel, and the reason it's there. Effort in words: "you can talk", "a few words". Heart rate only if you want it.

B — The week

Your rules hold.

Three runs, never two days in a row. Drag a session to another day and the plan checks your rules before it lets it land.

C — Progress

Proof, not points.

Hours on your feet, how much of it was truly easy, your longest run so far. Your watch data sits right below: load, easy share, climbing speed.

04 — Why it existsLondon · Gulf of Morbihan

Built for one race first.

I'm training for the Ultra Marin Grand Raid: 175 km around the Gulf of Morbihan, through the night, in June 2027. I wanted a coach that knew my week, my injury history, and the fact that I already ride a bike every day.

The apps I tried gave me charts. I wanted a plan, and a reason for every session in it. So I built one, and it's coaching me there first. When it has earned it, it opens to you.

Justin Seriat, founder
